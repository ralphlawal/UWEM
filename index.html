About Sky-Light

Established in 2009, Sky-Light Model College offers continuous education from Creche through College. Our mission is to provide holistic learning that builds character, academic excellence, and practical skills.

Our Mission: To nurture well-rounded students through quality education, discipline and moral values, preparing them to excel academically and socially.
